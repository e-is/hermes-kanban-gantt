# Kanban Gantt — promo (design spec)

## Style Prompt
An editorial, print-like product film: warm paper, one cobalt ink, one solar yellow,
and Playfair Display carrying every headline. Flat planes, hairline rules, no shadows,
no rounded cards. The subject is time, so the axis is the hero: thin rules, mono ticks,
bars that behave like printed marks.

## Colors
- paper: #EFE9DC (ground, every frame)
- ink: #16130F (all body type, hairlines at 20%)
- cobalt: #1F2BE0 (the one ink for display + the statement panel)
- sun: #F2E51F (accent panels, command prompt, highlights)
- ember: #DE5A38 (running state only)

## Typography
- display: Playfair Display 900 — headlines, 76–150px, tracking -0.02em
- body: Archivo 400/600 — 24–30px, line-height 1.45
- data: JetBrains Mono 400/500 — 15–18px, tracking 0.16em, uppercase for labels

## What NOT to Do
- No gradients, no drop shadows, no rounded corners above 2px.
- No stock icons in circles, no bullet slides, no three-card rows.
- Never center a paragraph; headlines may center only on the statement frame.
- Never put type over the screenshots' own UI chrome.
